What the zones mean
Fix it — your child feels they can do something about this worry. A small action or conversation might help.
Help a bit — your child knows they can't fix everything but want to contribute. Validation and support helps here.
Float away — your child recognises something is outside their control. Gently reinforcing this is healthy, not giving up.
Conversation starters
"I noticed you were thinking about some worries today — is there anything you'd like to talk about?"
"Sometimes worries feel smaller when we share them. I'm always here if you want to."
"You don't need to fix everything. Some things are just too big for any of us — and that's okay."
