How it works

Simple enough for little ones. Powerful enough to help.

Zephyr walks children through their worries one at a time — simple, gentle, no right or wrong answers.

☁️ Step 1
Pick your worry cloud

Choose from friendly categories — School, Friends, Family, Big Feelings, and more. Then pick which worry feels biggest right now.

Step 2
Zephyr thinks it over

Zephyr listens carefully and decides where the worry belongs — in a warm, gentle way that always feels kind.

☀️ Step 3
Watch your sky clear

Sort as many clouds as you like. At the end, see your clear sky — and feel a little lighter than before.

The three cloud jars

Every worry finds its place.

🌟
You can fix this!

Some worries are things you can do something about. Zephyr helps you see what that one small thing might be.

🤝
You can help a bit!

Some worries are too big to fix alone — but you can still do your part. That's always enough.

🎈
Let this one float away

Some worries aren't yours to carry. Zephyr helps you let them go — like a balloon drifting up into the sky.

A note for grown-ups.

Zephyr is built on the same Stoic philosophy and CBT framework as Circle of Control — the adult version used by thousands of people to manage stress and anxiety.

The worry sorting framework has been simplified for children aged 5–10, with choice-based options so no reading is required. Zephyr never tells children what to feel or what to do — only asks gentle questions and helps them see what's theirs to hold.

Nothing your child types is stored or shared. Everything stays on their device.
